Privacy policy

Noam-Dalia gate-access app for Android. Last updated 8 October 2026. This policy is under legal review and may be updated.

1. Who we are

Noam-Dalia is operated by Imry Rodkin ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your data: rodkin.imry@gmail.com.

2. Who this applies to

The app is used at sites — such as a hotel or a residential building — that have installed gates controlled by Noam-Dalia. Its users are the site's security staff and managers, visitors given a time-limited pass, and the installers who set the gates up. The organisation running the site decides who is given access to its gates.

3. What we collect, and why

DataWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in (we email you a one-time code) and to contact you about your account.
Your display nameSo site managers can see who has access.
Your phone number (optional; managers and installers)So the site can reach you. Not shared.

A gate-network account linkA random identifier, not a password you chose, that connects your account to the gate network (Tuya). It is held on our server where only our backend can read it, and in your phone's secure storage.

A report holds the app and Android version, the type and code location of the error and the hour it happened — never your name, email, gate, site, location or Wi-Fi network. You can see the full list in the app under Settings → Privacy → What's included.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps.

4. On your phone

  • Wi-Fi network name and password — only when an installer adds a new gate device, so the device can join the site's Wi-Fi. They are passed to the device and not stored by us.
  • Location permission — Android requires it to scan for Wi-Fi networks while pairing a device. It is used only for pairing, never to track where you are.
  • Gate permissions cached for up to 24 hours, so gates still open when the phone is offline.
  • Your sign-in session, kept in the phone's secure storage and excluded from phone backups and device transfers. Signing out removes it.

6. How long we keep it

When you delete your account, your profile, memberships, permissions and gate-network link are removed. Access log entries you were part of are kept until their 365 days run out, with your name and account removed from them. How to delete: Delete your account.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data: email us and we will send you a copy or correct it.
  • Delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account, or by email (see Delete your account).
  • Error reports: turn automatic sending on or off in Settings → Privacy.
  • If your access was given by your employer, some requests may also need to go through them, since they decide who has access to their site.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Access to each table on our server is restricted to the people who need it, the gate-network link can be read only by our backend, and the app stores its session in the phone's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you.

9. Children

The app is a workplace and building-access tool and is not directed at children.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app or by email.
