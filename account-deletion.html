Delete your account

You can delete your Noam-Dalia account and all associated data from inside the app: open Settings → Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your profile, building memberships, and access grants, and removes your Tuya Home access where that can be done automatically.

If you no longer have the app installed, or cannot sign in, you can request deletion by emailing rodkin.imry@gmail.com from the email address on your account. Include the building or site name so we can confirm your identity. Requests are processed within a few business days.

Deleting your account removes your profile, your membership in every building, and your access grants. If you administer a building alone, deleting your account also deletes that building and its full history, since no one would be left to read it. Otherwise, a record of gate-open events you were involved in is kept for up to 365 days for security and incident-review purposes, with your name and account removed from it once your account is deleted.
